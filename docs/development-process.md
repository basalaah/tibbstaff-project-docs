# Development Process and Team Collaboration

## 1. Team and Development Approach

tibbstaff.com was developed collaboratively by a team of two developers.

The team organized much of the development work around individual features rather than separating responsibilities strictly into frontend and backend roles. Each developer implemented assigned functionality and integrated it into the wider application.

This approach provided practical experience in feature development, application integration, and collaborative software development.

## 3. Specification-Driven Development

The project used GitHub Spec Kit to support a specification-driven development workflow.

This approach helped structure feature requirements and implementation planning before development. Specifications were used to organize functionality and clarify expected user behavior across key workflows, including authentication, role selection, dashboards, and recruitment activities.

Using this workflow provided practical experience with structured feature planning and translating requirements into application functionality.

## 4. Platform Evolution

The project originally began as a mobile application built with Flutter and Dart.

During development, the team encountered challenges publishing the application through Google Play. We subsequently moved toward a Progressive Web App (PWA) approach to make the platform accessible through supported web browsers and simplify distribution and application updates.

This decision addressed a practical distribution challenge while preserving the platform's core purpose: connecting healthcare professionals with hiring institutions.

## 5. Product Direction

The project began as a business idea. During development, the team decided to make the platform free to use.

This decision changed the initial business direction while retaining the objective of providing a platform where healthcare professionals could discover employment opportunities and institutions could manage recruitment activities.

The experience highlighted the relationship between technical development, product decisions, and the practical needs of the intended users.

## 6. Project Status

tibbstaff.com is live and has received user registrations, although overall traffic and adoption remain limited.

The project represents practical experience taking a recruitment platform from an initial idea through development and release. It also provides a foundation for continued improvement, user feedback, and evaluation of the platform's value to job seekers and hiring institutions.

## 7. Areas for Future Improvement

Potential areas for further development include:

- Refining onboarding and user experience.
- Evaluating usability and accessibility across supported devices.
- Improving application reliability and error monitoring.
- Gathering feedback from job seekers and hiring institutions.
- Increasing platform visibility and user adoption.
- Refining recruitment workflows in response to user needs.

These areas represent opportunities for future development rather than completed features.
