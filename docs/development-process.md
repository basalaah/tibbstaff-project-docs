# Development Process and Team Collaboration

## 1. Team Structure

tibbstaff.com was developed collaboratively by two developers.

Rather than dividing responsibilities exclusively between frontend and backend development, we organized much of the work around individual features.

Each developer was responsible for implementing assigned features across the frontend and backend, followed by integration into the wider application.

## 2. My Responsibilities

My individual contributions included:

- Authentication and authorization.
- User profile management.
- Job posting.
- Job search and filtering.
- Job application workflows.
- Dashboards for job seekers and hiring institutions.
- General frontend improvements across the application.

My role extended beyond assigned features through ongoing work to improve the frontend experience and maintain consistency across the wider application.

## 3. Specification-Driven Development

The project used GitHub Spec Kit as part of its development workflow.

The intent of specification-driven development is to establish requirements and expected behavior before implementation, then use structured specifications and plans to guide development.

The project specifications document key user journeys, including authentication, role selection, role-specific dashboards, and recruitment workflows.

## 4. Platform Evolution

The project initially started as a mobile application built with Flutter and Dart.

During development, we encountered difficulties with publishing the application through Google Play. We subsequently shifted toward a Progressive Web App to simplify distribution and make the platform accessible through supported web browsers across devices.

This change reflected a practical decision about distribution and maintenance rather than a change to the platform's core recruitment purpose.

## 5. Product Direction

The platform began as a business idea. During development, we decided to make it free to use.

This changed the initial business direction while preserving the goal of connecting healthcare professionals with hiring institutions.

## 6. Current Stage

The platform is live and has received user registrations, but overall traffic and adoption remain limited.

The project therefore represents hands-on experience with developing and releasing a real application, while also leaving room for further product validation, user acquisition, and improvement.

## 7. Lessons and Future Opportunities

Potential areas for continued development include:

- Improving the onboarding experience.
- Evaluating usability and accessibility across devices.
- Monitoring errors and application reliability.
- Gathering feedback from job seekers and hiring institutions.
- Improving discoverability and adoption.
- Refining recruitment workflows based on actual user needs.

These are potential improvement areas, not claims that the work has already been completed.
