# Development Process and Team Collaboration

## 1. Project Background

tibbstaff.com began as a business idea focused on connecting healthcare professionals with hiring institutions.

During development, the team decided to make the platform free to use rather than continue with the original business model.

The project was developed collaboratively by two developers.

## 2. Team Development Approach

We divided much of the development work by feature rather than assigning one developer exclusively to frontend work and the other exclusively to backend work.

Each developer was responsible for implementing assigned features across the frontend and backend, followed by integration with the wider application.

This approach allowed both developers to work across different parts of the application while contributing to the overall product.

## 3. My Individual Contributions

My responsibilities included:

- Authentication and authorization.
- User profile management.
- Job posting.
- Job search and filtering.
- Job application workflows.
- Dashboards for job seekers and hiring institutions.
- Improving the frontend experience and consistency across the wider application.

My work extended beyond assigned features through general frontend refinement and improvements across the application.

These responsibilities describe my contribution and should not be interpreted as a claim that I independently built the entire platform.

## 4. Specification-Driven Development

We used GitHub Spec Kit as part of our development workflow.

Specification-driven development helps structure implementation around documented requirements, expected behavior, planning, and tasks.

The project's specifications describe important user journeys, including authentication, role selection, job-seeker workflows, institution workflows, and application navigation.

## 5. Evolution from Mobile Application to PWA

The project initially started as a Flutter mobile application using Dart.

During development, we encountered difficulties with publishing the application through Google Play. We decided to shift toward a Progressive Web App to simplify distribution and make the platform accessible across supported devices through a web browser.

The goal was to reduce distribution and maintenance overhead while keeping the platform accessible without requiring users to obtain a separate native application.

## 6. Product Direction

Although the project began as a business idea, we eventually decided to make the platform free to use.

This changed the initial business direction while retaining the goal of connecting healthcare professionals and hiring institutions.

## 7. Current Stage

The platform is live and has received user registrations, although overall traffic and adoption remain limited.

This project represents practical experience collaborating on the development and release of a real application, working across frontend and backend features, handling role-based user experiences, and adapting the delivery approach to practical constraints.

## 8. Potential Areas for Further Improvement

Potential areas for future work include:

- Improving onboarding and profile completion.
- Validating the end-to-end job application lifecycle.
- Completing and testing notification functionality.
- Refining applicant document review and verification workflows.
- Improving usability across supported devices.
- Gathering user feedback and improving adoption.

These are potential improvement areas, not claims that the work has already been completed.
